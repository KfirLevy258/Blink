**BLINK 1.2.3 handles a desk with more than one board.** Two changes to how the app finds its board; the firmware is unchanged apart from its version.

## What changed

- **When the board is unplugged, the app looks for it again everywhere.** It used to assume the board would come back on the same port forever. Now a disconnect starts the search over: the last-used port first, then every other serial device, and whichever one answers is the board from then on -- so moving the board to another USB socket, or swapping in a different unit, just works.
- **`blink status` now shows the board.** It names the port the app is talking to, the board's id and firmware, and lists any other serial devices it saw and left alone. A developer with other CH340 boards on the desk can see at a glance that the app picked the right one.
- **No more double-boot on plug-in.** A board that was still starting up when the app first looked is now given the full listening window before the app decides it is wedged and resets it, so plugging in no longer makes the screen boot twice.

## Updating

The app updates itself; the screen then offers this firmware, and one tap installs it.

Something wrong? Email **support@blink-buddy.com** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
