**BLINK 1.1.2 keeps the screen's firmware current.** Two fixes to the "Software update" row.

## What changed

- **The screen learns about new firmware without being asked.** A device only checked for firmware once, when it started, and when you tapped the update row. A device left plugged in through several app updates never heard about the firmware that came with them. Now every time the app connects -- at login, after an app update, when the board is plugged in -- it checks, and the row shows "Install x.y.z" or "Up to date" on its own.
- **"Checking..." cannot get stuck.** If a tap on the update row landed while the app was restarting, the row stayed on "Checking..." with nothing answering. From this firmware on it gives up after thirty seconds, says so, and a tap asks again. The app-side fix above clears it on the next connect even on older firmware.

Nothing else changed.

## Updating

If you have 1.1.0 or newer, the screen offers this one; one tap installs both halves. Devices still on older firmware get the offer as soon as the new app connects -- which this release causes.

Something wrong? Email **support@blink-buddy.com** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
