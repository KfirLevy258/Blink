**BLINK 1.2.0 sleeps when your computer does.** A new face for the screen, and a hint for the one install problem we have actually seen.

## What changed

- **Sleep.** When your computer goes to sleep, the screen closes its eyes and dozes -- Zs and all -- for as long as the computer sleeps. When it wakes, the eyes open and the dials come back. It takes about thirty seconds after the computer stops answering; nothing to set up. A tap while it sleeps shows the last figures with a note that your computer may be asleep.
- **"Try another USB cable."** If the screen sits on "connecting" for twenty seconds with the app running, the likeliest cause is a charge-only cable, and the screen now says so.
- **Uninstall says goodbye.** Removing the app tells the screen, so it shows "connecting" rather than dozing over a computer that no longer has it.

Both editions get the same sleep face in their own colours. Company editions do not show the logo on waking -- it is a wake, not a start.

## Updating

The screen offers this one; one tap installs both halves.

Something wrong? Email **support@blink-buddy.com** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
