**BLINK 1.2.1 keeps Windows quiet.** One fix.

## What changed

- **No window during a firmware update on Windows.** The app runs hidden, but the tool it starts to write the firmware was given a console window of its own, so a black window appeared on the desktop for about a minute of every firmware update. Every process the app starts now stays hidden.

Nothing else changed; the firmware is unchanged apart from its version number.

## Updating

The screen offers this one; one tap installs it.

Something wrong? Email **support@blink-buddy.com** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
