**BLINK 1.2.5 reads correctly when you go past your weekly limit.** A gauge that emptied at the exact moment it should have been full, plus the reading's age on the panel.

## What changed

- **Going into extra usage no longer empties the ring.** Past your weekly limit, Claude reports more than 100% -- and the board only accepted numbers up to 100, so 102% arrived as **0%**. On a desk with two sources it was worse: one of them caps its own figure at 100 and drew a full ring, the other reported the truth and drew nothing, so the panel flipped between full and empty every minute. The board now takes the real number, shows **102%**, and holds the ring full because a ring has nowhere past full to go.
- **The app protects boards it has not updated yet.** A board still on older firmware is sent 100 rather than 102, so it draws a full ring instead of an empty one. Nothing to do: this half works the moment the app updates, before the board is touched.
- **Two other sources stopped throwing the number away.** The Claude Desktop and Codex readers treated anything above 100% as a corrupt field and reported "unknown", which drew `--%`. An over-limit reading is a reading.
- **The panel says how old a figure is.** When a reading is more than ten minutes old the page it belongs to says so, and a live page beside it stays clean. Claude Desktop only refreshes while you are at the machine, so an old figure usually means nobody was there -- the panel now says that instead of redrawing an hours-old number as if it just arrived.
- **`blink status` names the one action that refreshes Claude Desktop:** opening its menu-bar icon. Focusing the app window fetches nothing.

## Updating

The app updates itself; the screen then offers this firmware, and one tap installs it. The extra-usage fix is fully in place once the board takes the firmware.

Something wrong? Email **support@blink-buddy.com** with the output of `blink status` and the last lines of `bridge.log`.
